## 💡 What I Learned

- A **PASS** means a rule met its expected threshold and the data is ready to move forward.
- A **WARNING** means the data needs attention but may still be usable depending on the agreed threshold and business impact.
- A **FAIL** means a critical rule was broken and should block or be resolved before downstream use.
- Data quality is not only about finding errors; it provides evidence that a pipeline can be trusted.
- Quality checks should be designed as part of the pipeline, not added only at the end.

## ⚠️ Challenge and Fix

- **Challenge:** It was initially easy to treat every data issue as the same level of severity.
- **Fix:** Used clear severity rules: PASS for acceptable results, WARNING for issues to monitor, and FAIL for issues that require action before release.

## ✅ Key Assumptions

- Each validation rule has a defined expected result or acceptable threshold.
- Critical failures should be documented and assigned to the appropriate layer owner.
- Status labels are meaningful only when the team agrees on the rule and its severity.
