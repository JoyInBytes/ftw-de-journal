# Project - Lakehouse Ready

- **Snapshot:** 2026-09-02
- **Status:** Live
- **Demo:** [Open Lakehouse Ready](https://lakehouse-ready.ceejaybalansay23.chatgpt.site)

## In one sentence

A free interactive study guide for preparing for Databricks Data Engineer Associate and Professional certification exams.

## What I built

- Separate Associate and Professional learning paths.
- Exam-style diagnostic questions with explanations.
- Progress tracking, domain-level readiness signals, study recommendations, and practical lab ideas.
- Clear reminders to verify details against current official Databricks exam guides.

## Tools and skills

- Databricks platform concepts, learning design, technical research, question writing, and progress tracking.

## What I learned

- A useful study tool should diagnose weak areas, explain mistakes, and guide the learner toward focused practice.
- Certification content needs regular review because product names and exam objectives can change.

## One small next step

- [ ] Review the question bank whenever Databricks publishes a new official exam guide.
