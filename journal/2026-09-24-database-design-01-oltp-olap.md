# OLTP and OLAP

- **OLTP:** Online Transaction Processing for daily operations such as sales, payments, and account updates.
- **OLAP:** Online Analytical Processing for reports, trends, and business decisions.

| OLTP | OLAP |
|---|---|
| Current transactions | Historical and consolidated data |
| Simple, fast queries | Complex analytical queries |
| Supports operations | Supports decision-making |

**Key takeaway:** OLTP helps the business operate; OLAP helps the business analyze and improve.
